# Commercial Balanced Framework

## Principles

1. Contractual obligations have priority.
2. Partial fulfillment may justify proportional payment.
3. Good faith mitigates penalties.
4. Fraud increases penalties.
5. Unjust enrichment should be avoided.
6. Delays should be analyzed contextually.
7. Evidence hierarchy:
   - signed contracts
   - delivery logs
   - written communication
   - secondary claims
